Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide photo booths for quinceañeras and sweet 16s?

Yes — the Magic Mirror booth is a popular choice for quinceañeras and sweet 16 parties. We build a custom overlay with the honoree's name and colors for the night.

Can Momento Photo Booth work at corporate events and fundraisers?

Absolutely. We regularly staff corporate parties, brand activations, galas, and fundraisers, with custom overlays that can include your company or event logo.

How far in advance should I book a wedding photo booth?

We recommend booking 2-3 months ahead for weddings, especially during peak spring and fall season, though we'll always try to accommodate shorter notice.

What's included in a Magic Mirror photo booth rental?

Every package includes delivery, setup, a custom overlay design, props, and a friendly on-site attendant for the full rental period, plus unlimited prints and instant digital sharing.

Do guests get unlimited prints and digital copies?

Yes — every guest can print as many copies as they like, and can also get an instant digital copy by text, AirDrop, or QR code.
