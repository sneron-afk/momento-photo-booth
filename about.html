Who We Are

Making Moments Memorable

Momento Photo Booth exists to add a little sparkle to your event. We bring the Magic Mirror photo booth — a full-length, glowing LED mirror booth — to weddings, quinceañeras, sweet 16s, corporate events, fundraisers, and celebrations of every kind.

Every booth is delivered, set up, and staffed by a friendly attendant who keeps the line moving and the energy up — so you can enjoy your own event instead of managing a photo station.

Guests leave with a printed keepsake in hand and a digital copy on their phone within seconds — a memento of the night that lasts long after the last song plays.
